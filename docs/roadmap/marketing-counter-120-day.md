# Marketing counter — 120 days

**As of:** 2026-11-14. **Status:** started. MC-01 through MC-06 are done. The next package on this track is **MC-07**. Engage stays on its own plan; this track is the one in progress.

**Window:** Monday 16 November 2026 through Sunday 15 March 2027. About twenty marketing sessions: one evening a week, plus four extra evenings in the first month. The other evenings stay on the Engage 90-day plan.

**Job.** A shop counter. One shop, its offers, and the still, caption, and email on each offer. A scheduled Instagram row can still publish, so the calendar stays off the home until an offer is marked ready.

**Out of this plan.** Cart abandonment, send-gate work, new tables, video, and a restyle of every frozen Company OS room. Landing pages, popups, and documents stay inside the builder and get no new features. LinkedIn, X, blog, ads, and the metrics chat get no new screens.

## What must keep working

The truth-sheet product. Read-only price and claim. Materials, place, in frame, keep out. The ten looks. Format. The Ultra reference and its frame warning. The scene sentence Fal receives. The feed check. Carousel roles. Story frames. The reel cover plus storyboard, with no video file. The journey email with the unsubscribe line. A cart reminder creates no Instagram post. A product drop with no product does not start.
